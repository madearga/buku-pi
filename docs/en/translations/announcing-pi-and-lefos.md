# Announcing Pi & Lefos

[Earendil Inc.](https://www.earendil.com) is a public benefit corporation founded by Armin Ronacher and Colin Daymond Hanna in 2025. Earendil's mission is to strengthen human autonomy by building open software and protocols, bridging division and ignorance, and cultivating lasting joy and understanding. To that end, Earendil is proud to announce several pieces of news today:

- Earendil has acquired the open-source project [Pi](https://pi.dev). Pi was created by Mario Zechner and is an outstanding example of craft and taste in today's software. Pi is a [minimalist Agent inside OpenClaw](https://lucumr.pocoo.org/2026/1/31/pi/), as well as a widely adopted open-source coding Agent and Harness. Mario's steadfast commitment to openness and craft aligns closely with the goals behind Earendil's founding. With the acquisition complete, Mario Zechner becomes a significant shareholder and a member of the Earendil team.
- [Lefos](https://lefos.com) enters public Alpha today. This is our first step toward a long-term goal: building a machine entity capable of proving itself both highly capable and trustworthy. Lefos communicates with you and your fellow creators by email. You control this technology stack, and it lets you collaborate with others. Lefos helps turn your inbox into a command line for thoughtful work and communication. Users can now visit [Lefos.com](https://lefos.com) to join the waitlist.
- Earendil is proud to introduce our early backers: Accel (Daniel Levine), Balderton (Daniel Waterhouse), and the founders of n8n (Jan Oberhauser), OpenClaw (Peter Steinberger), Revolut (Vlad Yatsenko), Sentry (David Cramer), and Slack (Cal Henderson), among others. Thank you for the trust and support they have given us.
