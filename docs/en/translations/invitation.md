# An Invitation to Begin a Correspondence

> - **Original title** *An Invitation to Begin a Correspondence*
> - **Author** Armin Ronacher and Colin Daymond Hanna, Earendil `<rfc@earendil.com>`
> - **Publication date** 2026-01-18
> - **Original address** [earendil.com/posts/invitation](https://earendil.com/posts/invitation/)
> - **License note** Adapted and translated with permission from Earendil (*Adapted and translated with permission from Earendil.*)
> - **Translation license** [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/deed.zh-hans)

Hello:

We believe we should build in the open, sharing what we learn, what we build ourselves, and where we are heading.

If you too are curious about software that can strengthen human autonomy and bridge understanding, we very much hope you will join this journey. [Leave your email address](https://earendil.com/posts/subscribe/) and you will receive our writing: honest reflections on what we are building, why it matters, and what we are shaping together.

This writing is written by humans, not machines.

We welcome your comments. We work in Vienna, Austria — a city with good coffee, culture, and conversation. If you happen to pass through and would like to say hello, send an email to `rfc@earendil.com`.

Our door is wide open.

Warm regards,

Armin and Colin
