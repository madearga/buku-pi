# A Reflection on Our Announcement Today

> - **Original title** *A Reflection on our Announcement Today*
> - **Author** Armin Ronacher and Colin Daymond Hanna, Earendil `<rfc@earendil.com>`
> - **Publication date** 2026-04-08
> - **Original address** [earendil.com/posts/announcement-reflection](https://earendil.com/posts/announcement-reflection/)
> - **License note** Adapted and translated with permission from Earendil (*Adapted and translated with permission from Earendil.*)
> - **Translation license** [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/deed.zh-hans)

Less than a year ago, in Vienna in the spring, Armin and Colin met to write at *Vogel Kaffee*. By then we had already settled on a name — *Earendil* — but there was no company yet, and no office. We were standing at the very beginning, and it felt most natural to write down together why this company ought to exist.

Again and again we revised those sentences, weighing and questioning every word, until they finally took a shape like this:

*Earendil exists to strengthen human autonomy by building open software and protocols, bridging division and ignorance, and cultivating lasting joy and understanding.*

These are ambitious aims, but we take every part of them seriously. In a world that every day seems more divided and more violent, these words are sharp yet universally meaningful; in a world that every day seems more afraid, these words are firm yet reassuring. That is exactly what we intended.

Today we are proud to announce three pieces of news: first, *Earendil* has acquired [Pi](https://pi.dev), and Mario Zechner has joined *Earendil*; second, we are releasing [Lefos](https://lefos.com), a new kind of entity that aims at capability and credibility at once; third, we want to thank our early backers.

Before founding Earendil, the two of us spent nearly ten years at our previous jobs. We believe in the importance of [planting a tree](https://lucumr.pocoo.org/2026/3/20/some-things-just-take-time/), then tending it over time. Today's three announcements, each in its own way, all reflect that principle.

The first is Mario. Armin has known him since Mario was still a student. Over the years, Mario honed his craft by writing software. The birth of Pi — and later its acceptance by Earendil — came from one side, a long-term focus on honing a craft, and from the other, the trust built over many years between those two people.

Pi sits at the center of the hottest part of AI, and at the same time is an outstanding example of software sharpened by hand. It is hard to imagine anything more aligned with Earendil's core principles.

The second is *Lefos*. Lefos really began with this question:

*How can a machine entity win human trust? How should it be built so that trust keeps growing over years and even decades, rather than slowly eroding bit by bit?*

The power today's machines show cannot be denied. But to truly serve humans, we believe several core principles need rethinking.

First, these machines should be built in the open and on open protocols. Second, people should be able to tailor such an entity to their own preferences and limits. Finally, companies should not simply take users' trust for granted.

If you have ever been lucky enough to work with an assistant, you probably would not hand over all your email passwords on the first day. Trust has to be earned over time. Our principle is similar: we want Lefos to slowly prove to you that it is trustworthy. We hope it can do so, and we look forward to your input.

Last come our backers. Since its founding, Earendil has been backed by Accel (Daniel Levine), Balderton (Daniel Waterhouse), and the founders of n8n (Jan Oberhauser), OpenClaw (Peter Steinberger), Revolut (Vlad Yatsenko), Sentry (David Cramer), and Slack (Cal Henderson), among others.

The people who work with us are friends and mentors, colleagues and partners. All of them are committed to deep, long-term relationships; many have also devoted years to the institutions and companies they founded themselves. We are proud to walk alongside them.

——Armin and Colin
