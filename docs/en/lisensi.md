# License & Attribution

This page explains the license status of each kind of material on the site, so you know what you may do when quoting, copying, or republishing it.

## Site code and layout

The site code and its theme are released under the **MIT License**; the full terms are in the `LICENSE` file in the project repository. You may use, change, and share them, including for commercial purposes, as long as the original owner's copyright notice is kept.

## Indonesian edition text

The Indonesian text across the learning path, case studies, and reference guide was written for this edition. If you quote it, name the source page together with its link.

## Third-party material

Some pages contain third-party material that does **not** automatically fall under the licenses above:

- Earendil article translations in the licensed translations section follow the original authors' **CC BY 4.0**.
- Quoted tweets, screenshots, product images, and trademarks remain with their respective owners.
- Product and brand names mentioned here are used for identification and explanation, not as a claim of ownership.

## Reporting a license problem

Report it through the project repository's issue tracker, naming the page and the part concerned, so it can be reviewed and corrected if needed.
